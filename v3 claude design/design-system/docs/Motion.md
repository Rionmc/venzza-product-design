# Motion

**Product motion is quiet feedback.** Transitions last .12–.26s and move one surface or border step on hover. Toasts slide in and dialogs scale in slightly. `prefers-reduced-motion` turns all animation off.

**Brand motion is confident and kinetic**, but always purposeful and never busy:

1. **Arrive, don't appear.** Headlines and blocks reveal on scroll with a short, eased rise and fade. One beat, then still.
2. **Geometry with meaning.** When things move, they act out the idea: points connect and the diamond resolves.
3. **Confident, not frantic.** Use fewer, larger moves: 300–700ms with custom easing (`--mk-ease`, `--mk-dur` 420ms, `--mk-dur-slow` 680ms). Hover adds energy, not noise.

**Library:** scroll reveal (section and card entrances), parallax depth (heroes), draw-on connection (hero moments), magnetic CTA (primary CTAs only), 3D tilt (feature cards), ambient gradient (hero backgrounds), infinite marquee (logo walls) and text-mask reveal (statement headlines). The Brand · Motion card shows them.
