# Voice & tone

Venzza sounds like a sharp creative partner: bold, direct, culturally fluent, and always on the creator's side. Use short sentences and real verbs, with no corporate fog.

- **Bold and direct.** Say the thing. Lead with the benefit and cut the throat-clearing.
- **Culturally fluent.** Speak both creator and brand: current, but never trying too hard.
- **On their side.** Confidence, not hype. Back the creator, not ourselves.

| Say | Not |
|---|---|
| "Turn your audience into a sponsorship." | "Leverage your community to monetize brand partnerships." |
| "One place where the whole deal lives." | "An end-to-end platform solution for sponsorship lifecycle management." |

**In product UI** the same voice is quieter:

- Buttons are a verb plus an object ("Deploy project", "Save changes"), never "OK" or "Submit".
- Status words are short: Published, Draft, In review.
- Errors say what happened and how to fix it: "Step 3 exited with code 1. Check the logs."
