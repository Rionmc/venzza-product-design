# Brand & product dialects

Venzza is where creators and brands converge. **The idea is Points → Connection → Structure → Value:** connected points (sponsors, creators, audiences, opportunities) resolve into the V, and at their convergence the diamond forms. That diamond is the value created when the right elements meet.

## Two dialects, one DNA

Product and Brand share one identity: the mark, electric lime, the neutral spine, the name and the voice. They differ only in how loudly that identity is applied. Product optimizes for clarity and use; Brand optimizes for attention and feeling.

| Dimension | Product | Brand |
|---|---|---|
| Accent (lime) | CTAs and active states only | Surfaces, hero fields, headline type |
| Typography | `--font-product` / `--font-marketing`, functional scale | `--brand-display` (Space Grotesk), oversized display |
| Layout | Dense, grid-based, utilitarian | Editorial, asymmetric, poster-like |
| Motion | Subtle, feedback-driven | Kinetic: reveals, confident |
| Density | High (information) | Low (impact and space) |
| Voice | Plain, helpful, precise | Bold, direct, culturally fluent |

**Shared core, never changed:** the V-in-circle mark with its diamond convergence; the palette of ink, electric lime and paper; the always-lowercase wordmark; and the voice of Connection · Alignment · Value.

## Colour with volume

Lime always means "Venzza energy". Product spends it precisely, with one CTA per view. Brand spends it generously, across whole surfaces, lime-to-ink gradients and poster contrast. Same meaning, different volume, and never a second accent hue competing with it.

## Brand type

Brand headlines use **Space Grotesk** (`--brand-display`, loaded from Google Fonts with a Helvetica Neue fallback), set oversized and tight: Hero 72 / .98 / -.03em, Display 52 / 1.0 / -.02em, H1 34 / 1.05, H2 24 / 1.1. Body copy stays on the product sans. Space Grotesk is for the brand space only and never appears in product UI.

## Patterns and layouts

- **Gradients:** signal mesh, aurora, duotone, grain field and ink spotlight. They are soft and restrained, built from the palette with grain layered on.
- **Geometry:** diamond field, diamond lattice, connection network, constellation, node grid and lime glow. Each one traces back to points connecting into value.
- **Layouts:** statement hero, editorial split and big-number band.

The Brand component cards show each one live.
