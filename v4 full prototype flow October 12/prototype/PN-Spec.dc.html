03Rules

Saving & publishing

  • Autosave every field, debounced about 600ms. Status reads “Saving…”, then “All changes saved”; on failure “Couldn’t save · Retry”, and the edit stays in the field.
  • Drafts vs live: sponsors keep seeing the last published version. Each saved change adds its section to unpublishedChanges; the count shows on “Review & publish”.
  • Publish publishes the whole project at once, never one section. First publish of a draft reads “Publish project” and lists it on the marketplace.
  • Preview and review show unpublished content. Requests and contact buttons are disabled there.

Packages

  • Installments: each payment is the price divided evenly, rounded down to the cent; the last payment absorbs the remainder, so totals always equal the price.
  • Readiness checks (warnings): no price → step 3; nothing included → step 2; no description → step 1. Info: a deliverable in no package → library; price hidden → step 3. Each check’s Fix opens that step.
  • Deleting a package shows an Undo toast for 5 seconds.
  • Deleting a deliverable happens only in the library, says how many packages use it, and offers Undo. Editing one anywhere updates every package.
  • Package colour is assigned on create: the first unused colour in the palette, in order. Not user-editable for now.

Cover image

  • Store a focal point (focalX, focalY as percentages) and render with object-fit: cover; object-position: X% Y%. One position serves every crop.
  • Crops: project page 1440 × 364 (≈ 4:1); mobile 390 × 330; marketplace card 16:9.
  • Overlay on the project page: image at 80% opacity, then a gradient: clear for the top 22%, 45% canvas at 52%, 85% at 78%, solid at the bottom.
  • Upload: JPG or PNG up to 20 MB; recommend 2400px wide or more; show the low-resolution warning below 1600px.
  • Remove returns to the empty state; the project then shows the category’s default pattern.

Marketplace search & matching

  • Search covers project names, taglines, categories, places, creators and tags, only within the marketplace. Suggestions are grouped (Categories, Projects, Places, Creators); picking a category sets the pill and clears the text.
  • Match to lists active campaigns with dates, budget and tags. Drafts appear disabled with “Finish setup”. With no campaigns it offers New campaign.
  • Matching shows projects with a fit of 70 or more, sorted by fit. Projects whose cheapest package is above the campaign budget stay in the results, last, labelled “Above budget · from $X”.
  • While matched: the featured banner hides, a summary bar shows the campaign’s tags, ages and budget, cards show Fit and “Add to wishlist”, and the count reads “N matches within budget”.
  • States: skeleton cards while matching; empty states say what to clear or change.
  • Save to a wishlist is on every card as a full-width button below the price, always visible; the picker opens upward over the card. If one campaign fits (70+), or a campaign is chosen in Match to, the button reads “[Campaign ▾] Add”. Otherwise it reads “Add to wishlist ▾” and opens the picker.
  • The picker (popover) shows Best match with its Fit, then all campaigns including drafts, each with its project count. Each row has two round buttons: + adds (it turns into a green ✓; hover shows ✕ to remove, with Undo in the toast) and ↗ goes to that campaign. One project can sit on several wishlists (“On 2 wishlists”). “New campaign” takes a name, creates a draft and adds the project.
  • Long lists (6+ campaigns): a “Find a campaign” field appears at the top and is focused on open. Sections: On this project (already saved), Best match (top 1–2 fits), Recent (last 3 saved to), then All campaigns A–Z with drafts last. Ended campaigns are hidden behind “Show past campaigns (N)”. The list scrolls inside the picker at about 7 rows; the title, search and New campaign stay fixed. Typing filters to Results; Enter adds to the first result; with no exact match the picker offers “Create “[name]” and add”. ↑↓ move between rows.
  • After saving: the button reads “✓ Added to [campaign]”; a toast offers Undo and View campaign. Esc closes the picker and returns focus to its button.

Wishlist activity (no nav dot)

  • Header: saving a project gives “Campaigns” one soft lime pulse (about 1.2s), then nothing. No persistent dot or count; none with reduced motion.
  • Home: the campaign row shows “+ 2 added today” and its action reads “Review”, linking to the campaign board.
  • Campaigns list: the Wishlist cell shows the count with a small “+2 today” pill underneath.
  • Campaign board: cards saved since the last visit show an “Added today” tag under the image.
  • All three fade after about a day, so there is nothing to clear. The dot stays reserved for news the user didn’t cause (teammates, Venzza suggestions).

Creating campaigns & projects

  • Two ways in. “New campaign” / “New project” on the list pages opens the one-page form. Home’s “Start a new …” cards and the first-time empty states open guided setup.
  • Guided setup wording: first-time users see the intro (“Here’s how a campaign works”). Returning users skip it and land on step 1, labelled “Guided setup · Step 1 of 4”, with a link to the one-page form.
  • One-page form: Basics card, then “Add more details” (audience, budget, goal for campaigns; dates, organizer, reach for projects). Required: campaign name and dates; project name, category and location. Optional fields say so.
  • One save action (“Create campaign” / “Create project”) in the sticky bar and at the bottom; solid white, not lime. Clicking it with fields missing shows a summary, marks each field and focuses the first.
  • Import (right column) reads a brief, deck or media kit and fills the form for review; “Undo import” clears it.
  • After creating: a campaign opens its board with matching started; a project opens in the editor as a draft (“Publish project” disabled until there’s a package).

Navigation

  • Edit project opens Overview; each section’s Edit link on the project page opens that section.
  • Back to editing returns to the section you previewed from.
  • Leaving the editor never loses work, because everything is already saved.
  • The package editor keeps your place: previous/next moves between packages, and closing returns to Cards or Compare as you left it.

05Keyboard & accessibility

  • ⌘K / Ctrl K opens search; ✕ clears the query while typing; Esc closes.
  • Esc closes the package editor and library panel; focus returns to the button that opened them.
  • Opening the editor focuses its first empty required field.
  • Cover reposition: arrow keys move 2% (Shift: 10%), Enter saves, Esc cancels. The frame is a focusable slider with instructions in its label.
  • View switches (Cards/Compare, Project page/Mobile/Card) use aria-pressed.
  • Compare is an ARIA grid (role=table) with row and column headers; quantity cells are buttons with full labels.
  • Status (saving, toasts, warnings) is announced with role=status.
  • Colour is never the only signal: ticks, labels and text carry meaning; colour adds to it. Reduced motion turns transitions off.

06Layout & responsive

  • Header: full width, raised (lighter) surface, 24px side gutter aligned with the rail, avatar-only account, nav centred. The avatar menu lists name and email, then Home, Settings, Analytics, and Sign out (Home is there as well as the logo, for people who don’t expect the logo to be a link). Translucent: 84% opacity with a 20px blur behind it, so content shows through softly while scrolling. Solid when search is open, in browsers without blur support, and with “reduce transparency”. The blur sits on a layer behind the header so its dropdowns position normally.
  • Rail: 264px; collapses to icons below 1360px (5.12).
  • Content: max 1120px, 32px padding; forms use a main column plus a 300px tips column.
  • Filters, two kinds: status (All, Draft, Working, Published, Active) uses the grey segmented tabs with counts. Categories and tags use outlined pills, selected solid white, in a second row; never a dropdown. Marketplace categories use the same pills.
  • Package editor: full screen with a 56px margin; form column plus a 400px live sponsor card column.
  • Compare: first column pinned; scrolls horizontally when packages don’t fit.
  • Mobile (to design next): rail becomes a section picker; the editor becomes a full-height sheet.

Rules

  • No publish step. A campaign is the sponsor’s private plan, so every edit autosaves and applies at once. “Done” returns to the campaign page.
  • Status follows the dates (Not started → Active → Ended). Archived is the only status set by hand.
  • Budget = committed (signed contracts) + open offers + left. Creators never see it. Lowering it below committed + offers shows a warning but never changes contracts.
  • Above budget compares a project’s lowest package to the total budget. Those projects are labelled and listed last, or hidden when showAboveBudget is off.
  • Delete is blocked while a contract is active or an offer is open; the dialog lists what’s in progress and offers Archive. An empty campaign deletes after confirmation.
  • Duplicate copies the brief only, never projects or contracts.
  • Requests & negotiation: Choose a package opens the project’s packages over the campaign (Cards or Compare, same data as the project page). The request panel shows terms, an optional different price (delta vs listed), a note and the budget impact; over-budget requests warn but can still be sent. A deal is a thread of events (request, counter, message, accept, decline, withdraw). Whoever didn’t make the last priced move sees Accept · Counter · Decline; the other side can Withdraw. Counters can change price and inclusions (shown as “Changed”). Accept asks for confirmation, then creates the contract and deliverables and moves the card to Sponsored. Decline needs a reason and returns the project to the wishlist. Budget: committed = accepted prices; open offers = your latest offer per deal.
  • Deliverable viewer: full-screen over the campaign (same inset as Find projects). Stage on the left (image carousel, video, live embed, saved screenshot, email, PDF), details on the right (status, results, caption, brief checklist from Strategy, versions, conversation). ← → move between all 12 items; numbered pills jump within a group. Approve moves to the next item waiting; Request changes needs a note, posted to the conversation. Live links are embedded (oEmbed) and a screenshot is captured when the link is added, shown if the post is removed. Overdue items offer Nudge; the creator sees Upload new version and Add link instead.
  • Table thumbnails: the latest submitted media, with a count when there’s more than one; otherwise an icon for the type.
  • Find projects opens a panel over the campaign (inset 32 × 40px, focus in search, Esc or Done closes, page scroll locked). Results use the same fit score as the marketplace: 70%+ by default with “Show more under 70% fit”, above-budget last or hidden with “Within budget”. Add and remove write to the wishlist immediately; projects already negotiating or sponsored show their stage instead of Add. Closing shows “N projects added”.

Participant (creator) view

  • Same route, filtered server-side to the viewer’s own contract: shared brief, documents marked shared, their deliverables and payments.
  • Never sent to the client: budget, match quality, wishlist, other projects, documents not shared.
  • Reached from the creator’s project page (“Packages sold”) and from notifications. Breadcrumb is Projects › project › campaign.
  • Pass 2: request and negotiation thread (Accept / Counter / Decline) and deliverable review (Approve / Request changes). Buttons for these show a placeholder toast for now.
