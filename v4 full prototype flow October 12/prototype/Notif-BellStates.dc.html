Notification bell

The bell lives in the global header, so every screen gets the same behavior. A lime dot means something new you haven't seen. There's no number: one dot is enough to say "look here", and the count lives inside the panel.

RestNothing new. Plain bell in secondary text.
UnreadSomething arrived that you haven't seen. The dot stays until you open the panel.
ArrivingThe moment a new one lands: the bell gives one short ring and the dot pulses once. No motion when the user prefers reduced motion.
HoverTooltip says how many are unread, so you can decide whether to open it.
Keyboard focusStandard focus ring. Enter or Space opens; Esc closes and returns focus to the bell.

Seen vs. read

Opening the panel marks everything seen, so the dot clears. Each row stays unread (bold, lime marker) until you open it or choose Mark all as read.

Where things show up

Bell: everything. Home: only what needs a decision, plus a chip on the campaign or project it belongs to. Toast: only for something that needs you, and only while you're in the app.

Lime means new

Lime appears only as the bell dot and the unread markers. Every action in notifications is a secondary button, so the page’s one lime primary action keeps its weight.

Accessibility

The bell's label changes to "Notifications, new activity" when the dot is on. New arrivals are announced politely, never interrupting. Everything works by keyboard.
